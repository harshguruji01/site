HarshGuruJi adheres to the principle of data minimization. We only collect information that is strictly necessary to provide, optimize, and secure our educational and technical services:

  • Information You Voluntarily Provide: When you register for an account, subscribe to platform notifications, contact our support team through our web form, or apply to join our contributor network, we may request your name, email address, username, profile preferences, and any message content or attachments you submit.
  • Automated Log and Diagnostic Data: Like most modern online web platforms, HarshGuruJi follows a standard procedure of utilizing log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and optionally the number of clicks. These are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable. The purpose of the information is for analyzing trends, administering the site, tracking users' movement on the website, and gathering demographic information.
  • Client-Side Utility Input: When you use our developer tools (such as JSON Formatter, Base64 Converter, Password Generator, Word Counter, or BMI Calculator), all computations and conversions execute 100% locally within your client browser sandbox using JavaScript. Your input text, passwords, or computational data are never transmitted to our servers or stored in any remote database.

  • Request that a business delete any personal data about the consumer that a business has collected;
  • Request that a business that sells or shares a consumer's personal data, not sell or share the consumer's personal data. HarshGuruJi does not sell, trade, or rent personal data to third parties;
  • Request correction of inaccurate personal information;
  • Exercise these rights without experiencing discrimination or loss of platform service quality.

If you submit a verifiable request, we have one month to respond to you. If you would like to exercise any of these rights, please contact our team at info@webguruji.online.

  • The right to rectification: You have the right to request that we correct any information you believe is inaccurate or complete information you believe is incomplete.
  • The right to erasure: You have the right to request that we erase your personal data, under certain statutory conditions.
  • The right to restrict processing: You have the right to request that we restrict the processing of your personal data, under certain conditions.
  • The right to object to processing: You have the right to object to our processing of your personal data, under certain conditions.
