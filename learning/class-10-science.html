Learning Resources: Chemical Reactions

1. What is a Chemical Reaction?

A chemical reaction is a process in which one or more substances (reactants) are converted to one or more different substances (products). In a chemical reaction, a chemical change must occur.

Key Observations of a Reaction:

  • Change in state
  • Change in color
  • Evolution of a gas
  • Change in temperature

2. Chemical Equations

A chemical equation is the symbolic representation of a chemical reaction in the form of symbols and formulae.

2Mg + O₂ → 2MgO

Important Definition: Balanced Equation

An equation in which the number of atoms of each element on the reactant side is equal to the number of atoms on the product side is called a balanced chemical equation. This follows the Law of Conservation of Mass.
