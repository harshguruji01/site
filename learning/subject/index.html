CBSE Class 10 Science: Board Exam Blueprint, Unit Weightage & Study Guide

The NCERT Class 10 Science Curriculum is thoughtfully engineered to bridge foundational secondary science with advanced higher secondary disciplines (Physics, Chemistry, and Biology). Under the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE) assessment guidelines, the annual board theory examination carries 80 marks, supported by 20 marks of internal school assessments (practicals, periodic tests, and portfolio activities). 2. High-Yield Board Revision Strategies

Master Chemical Balancing

Always practice the algebraic or hit-and-trial balancing method. State symbols (solid (s), liquid (l), gas (g), aqueous (aq)) and catalyst conditions above the yield arrow carry explicit grading marks.

Ray Optics & Sign Conventions

Strictly follow the Cartesian sign convention for spherical mirrors and lenses. Diagrams must clearly illustrate incident ray arrows and virtual image dashed extensions.

Biological Flowcharts

Memorize step-by-step pathways (e.g. breakdown of glucose via aerobic vs anaerobic respiration, nephron filtration in kidneys, and double circulation in the human heart).

Circuit Law Formulas

Ohm's Law ($V = IR$), series vs parallel equivalent resistance ($R_s = R_1 + R_2$,

/R_p = 1/R_1 + 1/R_2$), and Joule's heating effect ($H = I^2Rt$) account for crucial numerical problems.
