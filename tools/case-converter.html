Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is the difference between Title Case and Sentence case?

In Sentence case, only the first letter of the first word and proper nouns are capitalized (just like a standard sentence). In Title Case, all major words (nouns, verbs, adjectives, adverbs) are capitalized while minor words (like "and", "or", "in", "to") remain in lowercase unless they start the headline.
