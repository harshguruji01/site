How Reading and Speaking Times Are Estimated

Cognitive research demonstrates that the average adult reads comfortably at approximately 200 to 250 words per minute (WPM) when reading silently. When presenting aloud at a podium or in a podcast, public speaking cadence slows to approximately 130 to 150 words per minute. You can estimate presentation duration using these standard benchmarks:

  • 5-Minute Speech: ~650 to 750 words
  • 10-Minute Presentation: ~1,300 to 1,500 words
  • 30-Minute Keynote: ~4,000 to 4,500 words

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Does this word counter store or log my text?

No. HarshGuruJi processes your text entirely in client memory within your web browser. Nothing is logged, transmitted, or saved to any external database.

How are hyphenated words counted?

Hyphenated terms such as "state-of-the-art" or "well-being" are standardly evaluated based on whitespace separators. Our algorithm adheres to international publishing standards to ensure compatibility with Microsoft Word and Google Docs.
