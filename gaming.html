Performance Engineering

🚀 The Definitive FPS Boost & Latency Optimization Guide

Tested, proven Windows 10/11 and Android tweaks to eliminate micro-stutters, reduce input lag, and unlock your monitor's true refresh rate.

01

Enable Windows Game Mode & HAGS

Turn on Windows Game Mode to prioritize CPU & GPU threads for your game. Enable Hardware-Accelerated GPU Scheduling (HAGS) to offload VRAM management directly to the GPU processor.

Settings > Gaming > Game Mode > Turn ON
02

NVIDIA Reflex & Ultra Low Latency

In NVIDIA Control Panel, set Low Latency Mode to Ultra or enable NVIDIA Reflex Low Latency + Boost in-game. This caps pre-rendered frames to reduce render queue lag.

NVIDIA Control Panel > Manage 3D Settings > Low Latency Mode
03

Verify Monitor Hertz (144Hz / 240Hz)

Over 40% of gamers inadvertently run high-refresh monitors at default 60Hz. Verify your Display Adapter properties and use a genuine DisplayPort (DP) 1.4 or HDMI 2.1 cable.

Settings > System > Display > Advanced Display > Refresh Rate
04

Expand Shader Cache Size (10 GB)

Micro-stutters frequently occur when shaders compile on the fly. Expand NVIDIA Shader Cache size from default (Driver Default) to 10 GB or Unlimited to prevent shader compilation drops.

Shader Cache Size: Set to 10 GB
05

Eliminate High Ping & Packet Loss

Always game over wired Cat6 Ethernet rather than 2.4GHz Wi-Fi. Switch to fast gaming DNS servers (Cloudflare 1.1.1.1 or Google 8.8.8.8) and disable bandwidth-hogging torrent/cloud sync apps.

Primary: 1.1.1.1 | Secondary: 1.0.0.1
06

Android RAM Plus & Thermal Throttle

On Android phones (Samsung/OnePlus/Xiaomi), disable Virtual RAM (RAM Plus)! Virtual RAM uses slow flash storage which causes severe frame drops during intense teamfights.

Settings > Battery & Device Care > Memory > RAM Plus: OFF

Knowledge Base

📖 Gaming Tech Jargon & Frequently Asked Questions

Demystifying modern gaming technologies so you make informed hardware and software decisions.

What is DLSS, FSR, and XeSS?

These are AI and temporal upscaling technologies developed by NVIDIA (DLSS), AMD (FSR), and Intel (XeSS). They render the game internally at a lower resolution (e.g., 1080p) and upscale it to 1440p or 4K with deep learning models, doubling your frame rates with virtually no perceptible loss in image fidelity.

What is the difference between V-Sync, G-Sync, and FreeSync?

Screen tearing occurs when your GPU's framerate falls out of step with your monitor's refresh cycles. Traditional V-Sync eliminates tearing but introduces massive input lag. G-Sync (NVIDIA hardware module) and FreeSync (AMD open standard) adapt your monitor's refresh rate dynamically to match your GPU's exact output in real time without lag.

Is 16 GB RAM still sufficient for gaming in 2026?

16 GB (Dual-Channel) remains the solid baseline for pure esports gaming (Valorant, CS2, BGMI on emulator). However, for modern AAA titles like Black Myth: Wukong, Cyberpunk 2077, Hogwarts Legacy, or multi-tasking with Discord and Chrome open, 32 GB RAM is now strongly recommended to prevent paging to disk.

Should I install games on an HDD or NVMe SSD?

Never install modern games on mechanical hard drives (HDD)! Modern games utilize streaming assets (DirectStorage, virtual textures) that require sustained read speeds above 2,500 MB/s. Installing on an HDD leads to severe texture pop-in, 60-second load times, and violent stuttering.

What is Mouse Polling Rate (1000Hz vs 4000Hz / 8000Hz)?

Polling rate measures how many times per second your mouse reports its position to your CPU. Standard gaming mice operate at 1000Hz (1ms delay). Modern competitive mice offer 4000Hz (0.25ms) or 8000Hz (0.125ms), providing visibly smoother cursor tracks on 240Hz+ monitors at the cost of higher CPU consumption.

How can I safely download games without viruses?

Only download games from verified digital distributors: Steam, Epic Games Store, GOG, Battle.net, Riot Client, Xbox App, and official Google Play Store. Never download "cracked", "compressed", or pirated game archives; over 85% of pirated gaming downloads bundle crypto-miners, rootkits, or credential stealers that compromise your Google and banking accounts.
